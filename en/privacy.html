Website privacy policy

This is a courtesy translation. The French version is the legally binding one.

This page covers the MKN Studio showcase website only. Each product (Vitanimo, Mitoya, Borne) has its own privacy policy.

What the site collects

The site sets no cookies without your consent. The quote request form sends the details you enter (name, email, phone, project description…) to the Web3Forms service, which forwards them by email to MKN Studio. They are used only to reply to your request and prepare a quote, and are neither sold nor used for marketing. Web3Forms keeps a submission history for up to 30 days. If you write to the contact address, your email is used only to reply to you and is never passed on to third parties.

Audience measurement (Google Analytics)

If you accept it through the banner shown on your first visit, the site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to measure its traffic: pages viewed, visit duration, device type, country and traffic source. These statistics are used only to improve the site. They are neither sold nor used for advertising.

Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) lasting up to 13 months. Data is kept for 14 months in Google Analytics. As Google is a US company, data may be transferred to the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and nothing is sent to Google. Your choice (accept or decline) is stored in your browser for 13 months. You can change it at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

Hosting and server logs

The host (GitHub Pages) may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. MKN Studio has no access to this data.

Fonts

Fonts are hosted with the site: no request is made to Google Fonts when pages load.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to, correction or deletion of your data by writing to contact@mknstudio.fr. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).
